Why Worship Hanuman Ji?

Shri Hanuman — Bajrang Bali, Pavanputra, Sankat Mochan — is the embodiment of strength, wisdom, humility and devotion to Shri Ram. Devotees remember him to overcome fear, gain courage and remove obstacles. Tuesday (Mangalvar) and Saturday (Shanivar) are his special days. A simple daily practice is to recite the Hanuman Chalisa, chant "ॐ हं हनुमते नमः" on a mala, and end with the Hanuman Aarti. For bigger occasions, devotees recite the Sundarkand, Bajrang Baan and Sankat Mochan Ashtak.
