About the Hanuman Aarti

"Aarti Kijai Hanuman Lala Ki" (आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की) is the most widely sung aarti of Lord Hanuman. It praises Hanuman Ji as the destroyer of evil and the strength of Shri Raghunath, and recalls his great deeds — crossing the ocean to Lanka, finding Mata Sita, burning Lanka, bringing the Sanjeevani herb to save Lakshman, and defeating Ahiravan in Patal Lok. The aarti is usually sung at the end of a Hanuman puja, after the Hanuman Chalisa.

Hanuman Aarti Lyrics in Hindi & English

टेक · Refrain#

आरती कीजै हनुमान लला की। दुष्ट दलन रघुनाथ कला की॥

Aarti keejai Hanuman lala ki, Dusht dalan Raghunath kala ki.

पद 1 · Verse 1#

जाके बल से गिरिवर काँपे। रोग दोष जाके निकट न झाँके॥

Jaake bal se girivar kaampe, Rog dosh jaake nikat na jhaanke.

पद 2 · Verse 2#

अंजनि पुत्र महा बलदाई। संतन के प्रभु सदा सहाई॥

Anjani putra maha baldaai, Santan ke prabhu sada sahaai.

पद 3 · Verse 3#

दे बीड़ा रघुनाथ पठाए। लंका जारि सिया सुधि लाए॥

De beeda Raghunath pathaaye, Lanka jaari Siya sudhi laaye.

पद 4 · Verse 4#

लंका सो कोट समुद्र सी खाई। जात पवनसुत बार न लाई॥

Lanka so kot samudra si khaai, Jaat Pavansut baar na laai.

पद 5 · Verse 5#

लंका जारि असुर संहारे। सियाराम जी के काज सँवारे॥

Lanka jaari asur sanhaare, Siyaram ji ke kaaj sanvaare.

पद 6 · Verse 6#

लक्ष्मण मूर्छित पड़े सकारे। आणि संजीवन प्राण उबारे॥

Lakshman moorchhit pade sakaare, Aani Sanjeevan praan ubaare.

पद 7 · Verse 7#

पैठि पताल तोरि यमकारे। अहिरावण की भुजा उखारे॥

Paithi paataal tori yamkaare, Ahiravan ki bhuja ukhaare.

पद 8 · Verse 8#

बाईं भुजा असुर दल मारे। दाईं भुजा संतजन तारे॥

Baayin bhuja asur dal maare, Daayin bhuja santjan taare.

पद 9 · Verse 9#

सुर नर मुनि जन आरती उतारें। जय जय जय हनुमान उचारें॥

Sur nar muni jan aarti utaare, Jai jai jai Hanuman uchhaare.

पद 10 · Verse 10#

कंचन थार कपूर लौ छाई। आरती करत अंजना माई॥

Kanchan thaar kapoor lau chhaai, Aarti karat Anjana maai.

पद 11 · Verse 11#

जो हनुमान जी की आरती गावे। बसि बैकुण्ठ परम पद पावे॥

Jo Hanuman ji ki aarti gaave, Basi Vaikunth param pad paave.

How to Perform Hanuman Ji's Aarti

  • Clean the puja place and offer flowers, sindoor and a diya before Hanuman Ji's image.
  • Light camphor (kapoor) or a ghee lamp in the aarti thali — "कंचन थार कपूर लौ छाई".
  • Rotate the thali clockwise in front of the deity while singing the aarti with family.
  • Ring the bell, offer prasad (boondi, laddoo or gud-chana), and take the aarti's warmth with both hands.
  • Tuesday and Saturday evenings are especially auspicious for Hanuman aarti.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most popular Hanuman Aarti?

'Aarti Kijai Hanuman Lala Ki, Dusht Dalan Raghunath Kala Ki' is the most popular Hanuman aarti, sung in temples and homes across India.

When should the Hanuman Aarti be sung?

It is sung at the end of Hanuman puja — usually in the morning and at evening sandhya. Tuesdays and Saturdays are considered especially auspicious.

What is the meaning of 'Aarti Kijai Hanuman Lala Ki'?

It means 'Let us perform the aarti of beloved Hanuman' — the one who destroys the wicked and is the power (kala) of Shri Raghunath (Lord Ram).
