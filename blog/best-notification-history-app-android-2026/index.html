What Is a Notification History App?

A notification history app automatically captures and stores all your incoming notifications in one organized place — before they disappear from your status bar. Instead of opening multiple apps repeatedly, you simply open the app and browse a complete, searchable timeline of every alert your phone has received.

  • Track all important updates in one dashboard
  • Find any missed or dismissed notification instantly
  • Recover accidentally cleared alerts and OTPs
  • Stay organized without constantly checking every app
  • Search through notification history by keyword or app
