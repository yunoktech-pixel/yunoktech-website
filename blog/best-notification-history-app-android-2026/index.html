What Is a Notification History App?

A notification history app is an Android utility that automatically captures and stores all your incoming notifications in one organized place — before they disappear from your status bar.

Instead of opening multiple apps repeatedly or trying to recall what a notification said, you simply open the notification history app and scroll through a complete, searchable timeline of every alert your phone has received.

Track all important updates in one dashboard
Find any missed or dismissed notification instantly
Recover accidentally cleared alerts and OTPs
Stay organized without constantly checking every app
Search through notification history by keyword or app
