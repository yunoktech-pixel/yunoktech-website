11 Benefits of Reading Hanuman Chalisa Daily

Written by Goswami Tulsidas, the 40 chaupais of the Hanuman Chalisa have been recited by millions of devotees for centuries. Here is what devotees experience from a daily path — and a simple routine you can start tomorrow morning.

What Is the Hanuman Chalisa?

The Hanuman Chalisa is a devotional hymn in Awadhi composed by Goswami Tulsidas. "Chalisa" comes from chalis — forty — for its 40 chaupais, framed by two opening dohas and one closing doha. It praises Hanuman Ji's strength, wisdom and devotion to Shri Ram.

11 Benefits Devotees Describe

  • 🧘1. Mental peace — rhythmic recitation calms a restless mind and slows the breath, much like meditation.
  • 💪2. Courage and confidence — "Bhoot pishach nikat nahi aave" reminds devotees that fear has no place near Hanuman's name.
  • 🛡️3. A sense of protection — many recite it before travel, exams or a difficult day.
  • 🌅4. A positive start to the day — ten focused minutes in the morning set the tone for everything after.
  • 🎯5. Better focus — memorising and reciting 40 verses trains attention and memory.
  • 😌6. Relief from stress and anxiety — devotion gives the mind something steady to hold on to.
  • 🙏7. Deeper bhakti — Hanuman Ji is the ideal devotee; the Chalisa teaches humble service.
  • 🪐8. Traditionally recited for Shani dosh — many devotees read it on Saturdays for relief during Sade Sati.
  • 🏠9. A peaceful home atmosphere — families reciting together create a shared, calm ritual.
  • 🧠10. Discipline — a daily path builds a habit of consistency that spills into other areas of life.
  • ❤️11. Gratitude and humility — the hymn turns attention away from worries and towards faith.

📿 These are spiritual and emotional benefits shared by devotees through tradition. The Chalisa is a practice of faith and is not a substitute for medical advice or treatment.

Best Time to Read Hanuman Chalisa

Brahma Muhurat (about 4–6 AM) or after your morning bath is considered most auspicious. Evening, after sunset with a diya lit, is also a popular time. Tuesday and Saturday are Hanuman Ji's special days.

How Many Times Should You Read It?

CountWhen devotees choose it
1 timeDaily routine — perfect for beginners
3 / 7 timesTuesdays and Saturdays

108 timesHanuman Jayanti or a sankalp (vow) — often done with family over a day

Simple Path Vidhi (Method)

  1. Bathe and wear clean clothes. Sit facing east or north.
  2. Light a diya (ghee or sesame oil) in front of Hanuman Ji or Shri Ram's image.
  3. Begin with Shri Ram's name or "Shri Guru Charan Saroj Raj…".
  4. Recite slowly and clearly — understanding the meaning matters more than speed.
  5. Finish with the Hanuman Aarti and offer prasad (boondi, gud-chana or banana).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can women read Hanuman Chalisa?

Yes. Anyone can recite the Hanuman Chalisa with devotion, irrespective of gender or age.

Can I read Hanuman Chalisa at night?

Yes. Many devotees recite it at night, especially before sleeping, for peace of mind and a sense of protection.

How long does it take to read Hanuman Chalisa once?

Around 7 to 10 minutes at a calm pace. With practice it becomes a short, steady morning routine.
