Remove a Photo Background on Android

Product photos, profile pictures, stickers, thumbnails and passport photos all look better with a clean background. You don't need a computer. Here's how to do it on your phone.

Founder, Yunok Tech · Android Developer

PopCut – Background Remover

Free · On-device AI · No sign-up

GET IT ONGoogle Play

Quick answer: To remove a photo background on Android, open a background remover app such as PopCut, pick a photo, tap to remove the background, refine the edges with the erase and restore brush, then save it as a transparent PNG or add a new background. PopCut runs the AI on your phone, so photos are not uploaded.

Key facts

  • Steps: pick photo → remove background → refine → save
  • Transparent background: save as PNG
  • Passport size: 35 × 45 mm on white
  • PopCut: on-device AI, no sign-up, 30+ filters

Why Remove a Background?

🛍️ Product photos👤 Profile pictures🪪 Passport photos🎬 YouTube thumbnails😄 Stickers🏷️ Logos

Some phone gallery apps now let you lift a subject out of a photo, but the options depend on your phone brand and model. A background remover app works the same way on any Android phone, and lets you add a new background, filters and text in the same place.

How to Remove a Background with PopCut

  1. Install PopCut free from Google Play and open it.
  2. Pick a photo from your gallery or take a new one with the camera.
  3. Tap to remove the background. The AI cuts out people, pets and products.
  4. Refine the edges by hand with the erase and restore brush if needed.
  5. Add a new background: a solid colour, a studio backdrop, a soft blur or any picture from your gallery.
  6. Save as a transparent PNG or JPG, or share straight to Instagram, WhatsApp or Facebook.

🔒 Privacy: PopCut removes backgrounds with AI that runs on your phone. Your photos are not uploaded to a server, and you don't need to sign up.

Tips for a Clean Cut-out

  • 💡Use good, even light. Shadows and dark corners make edges harder to detect.
  • 🎨Pick a contrasting background when you shoot. A dark object on a light wall cuts out cleanly.
  • 🧍Keep the subject sharp. A blurry outline gives a blurry cut-out.
  • 💇Zoom in on hair and fur. Use the restore brush for fine detail the AI missed.
  • 📁Save as PNG for transparency. JPG doesn't support transparent backgrounds.

Make a Passport-Size Photo on Your Phone

PopCut has ready-made sizes for 35 × 45 mm passport photos and 2 × 2 inch ID photos on a white background, plus 4 × 6 and 5 × 7 inch prints at 300 DPI.

  1. Stand in front of a plain wall in good, even light. Face the camera with a neutral expression.
  2. Remove the background and choose a white background.
  3. Pick the passport or ID size and adjust the crop.
  4. Save it and print it at a photo shop.

📋 Photo rules are set by each passport or visa office, and they can change. Always check the current requirements for size, background and expression before you print.

Resize for Social Media

After editing, PopCut can resize your photo to exact sizes for Instagram posts, stories and reels, YouTube thumbnails, Pinterest, WhatsApp status and DP, and more. Add 30+ filters, stylish text with 25+ fonts and stickers to finish the look.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove the background from a photo on Android?

Use a background remover app like PopCut. Pick a photo, tap to remove the background, refine the edges if needed, then save it or add a new background.

How do I save a photo with a transparent background?

Save the cut-out as a PNG. PNG supports transparency; JPG does not.

Are my photos uploaded when I use PopCut?

No. Background removal runs on your phone, and your photos are not uploaded to a server.

Can I make a passport photo on my phone?

Yes. PopCut has 35 × 45 mm passport and 2 × 2 inch ID sizes on a white background. Check your passport office's current photo rules before printing.
