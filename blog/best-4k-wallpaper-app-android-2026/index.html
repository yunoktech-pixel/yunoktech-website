What Makes a Great Wallpaper App?

A wallpaper is the one thing you see every time you pick up your phone — easily 80+ times a day. A good wallpaper app should make it effortless to find something beautiful, see how it will look, and set it without fuss.

The 7-Point Checklist

  • 🖼️True high resolution. Images should be sized for modern tall screens (20:9 and taller), not stretched old 16:9 images.
  • 👁️Preview before setting. You should see the wallpaper on a lock-screen and home-screen mock with your clock and icons.
  • 🎚️Adjustments. Dim, blur and position controls make busy images work behind icons.
  • 🔒Separate lock and home. Set one, the other, or both.
  • 🔄Automatic changer. A fresh wallpaper every day without opening the app.
  • 📶Data-friendly. Caching and a Wi-Fi-only option so it doesn't eat your mobile data.
  • 🛡️Respectful privacy. No forced sign-up, clear consent choices for ads and analytics.
