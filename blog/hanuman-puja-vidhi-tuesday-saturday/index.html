Hanuman Puja Vidhi at Home on Tuesday & Saturday

You don't need a temple or a priest to worship Hanuman Ji. With a clean corner, a diya and a sincere heart, you can do a complete puja at home in about 30 minutes. Here is a simple routine for Tuesdays and Saturdays.

Why Tuesday and Saturday?

Tuesday (Mangalvar) is traditionally associated with Hanuman Ji's birth and with Mangal (Mars) — devotees worship him for strength and courage. Saturday (Shanivar) is linked to the legend of Hanuman Ji freeing Shani Dev, so devotees pray to him for relief from Shani's difficult phases.

Puja Samagri (What You Need)

🖼️ Hanuman Ji's image or idol 🪔 Diya with ghee or mustard / sesame oil 🔴 Red or orange cloth 🧡 Sindoor & chameli oil 🌺 Red flowers 🌿 Tulsi leaves 🍌 Prasad — boondi, gud-chana, banana 🪷 Agarbatti / dhoop

Step-by-Step Puja Vidhi

  1. Prepare. Bathe, wear clean (ideally red or orange) clothes and clean the puja space.
  2. Set up. Place Hanuman Ji's image on a red cloth, facing you as you sit towards east or north.
  3. Light the diya and agarbatti. Remember Shri Ram and Mata Sita first — Hanuman Ji is pleased by Ram bhakti.
  4. Offer sindoor mixed with a little chameli oil, red flowers and tulsi.
  5. Recite the Hanuman Chalisa — once, or 3, 7 or 11 times.
  6. Add Bajrang Baan or Sankat Mochan Hanuman Ashtak if you are facing a particular difficulty.
  7. Mantra Jaap. Chant "Om Hanumate Namah" or "Om Shri Hanumate Namah" 108 times on a mala.
  8. Aarti. Sing "Aarti Kije Hanuman Lala Ki" and offer prasad.
  9. Pray with folded hands, share prasad with family.

Tuesday vs Saturday — Small Differences

Tuesday (Mangalvar)Saturday (Shanivar)
DiyaGhee diyaMustard or sesame oil diya
Common offeringBoondi, besan laddooGud-chana, black urad items
Popular pathChalisa, SundarkandChalisa, Bajrang Baan, Sankat Mochan
IntentionStrength, courage, successProtection, relief from Shani dosh

Things to Keep in Mind

  • 🙏Devotion matters more than perfect rituals — a sincere one-time Chalisa is better than a rushed eleven.
  • 🥗Many devotees keep the day sattvik — no non-veg, alcohol or onion-garlic.
  • 🕯️Never leave a lit diya unattended.
  • 📿Use a digital mala counter if you lose count during jaap.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do Hanuman puja at home without a priest?

Yes. A simple home puja with a diya, flowers, the Hanuman Chalisa and Aarti is a complete and sincere form of worship.

Which mantra should I chant on Tuesday?

Om Hanumate Namah or Om Shri Hanumate Namah, chanted 108 times on a mala, is a simple and popular choice.

Should I read Bajrang Baan every day?

Many devotees recite Bajrang Baan on Tuesdays and Saturdays or during difficult times, alongside the daily Hanuman Chalisa.

How long does the full puja take?

About 25 to 40 minutes, depending on how many times you recite the Chalisa and whether you add Bajrang Baan or Sundarkand.
