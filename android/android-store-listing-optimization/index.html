App Store Listing Optimization for Android

Your store listing is the page where a visitor decides in seconds whether to install. App store optimisation (ASO) on Google Play means being findable for the right searches and convincing once found, without misleading metadata that violates policy.

Two apps with similar quality can see very different installs, because one listing clearly communicates value and matches what people search for, while the other shows a generic icon and a wall of text. ASO is product communication, measured.

The listing funnel

Title and descriptions

  • App name (limited length): brand plus a clear descriptor, for example "NotiVault: Notification History".
  • Short description: one clear benefit sentence, which users see before expanding.
  • Full description: lead with the problem you solve, then key features as short paragraphs or bullets, then trust signals (privacy, offline, no account needed).
  • Use the words your users would search for, naturally. Keyword stuffing, competitor names, misleading claims and ranking or price promotions in metadata violate Play policy.

Icon, screenshots and video

  • Icon: simple, recognisable at small sizes, distinct from competitors, and following Play's icon specifications.
  • Screenshots: show real app screens with short benefit captions. Put the most compelling value first, since many users only see the first two or three.
  • Feature graphic and video: optional but useful. Keep videos short and show the app in use.
  • Include tablet and other form-factor screenshots if you support them.

Localisation

Translate the listing for your main markets, and localise screenshots (UI language, culturally relevant examples). In India, consider Hindi and other regional languages if your app supports them. Users convert better in their language.

Store listing experiments

  1. Pick one element to test: icon, first screenshot or short description.
  2. Create variants in Play Console → Store listing experiments.
  3. Run until results are statistically meaningful, then apply the winner.
  4. Test again. Small improvements compound.

Custom store listings

Create listings for specific countries, or for traffic from specific campaigns (pre-registration, ads), with tailored messaging and screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

Does the description affect search on Google Play?

Play uses listing text, among many signals, to understand your app. Write clearly for users, and include relevant terms naturally.

How often should I update screenshots?

Whenever the UI changes noticeably or a new key feature ships. Outdated screenshots erode trust.

Can I mention "#1" or "best"?

Play's metadata policy restricts unverifiable ranking claims and promotional terms in titles and icons. Check the current policy.

Conclusion

Good ASO combines clear, policy-compliant text, benefit-first visuals, localisation and continuous experiments, backed by an app that retains users. Measure each funnel stage, and improve the weakest one first.
